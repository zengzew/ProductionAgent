# Story Bible — episode-004: Devin / Cognition

## Working title
AI 软件工程师 Devin：把任务交给它，看着它干完

## Central idea
Devin is an AI software engineer teammates can hand tasks to. It works in a visible workspace with browser, code editor and terminal, then returns the finished task for human review. The official launch demo got attention; pricing widened access; a Mercedes case and a $1B+ round at $2.5B pre-money gave a market price.

## Core story question
Cognition 的创始人抓住了哪个需求，Devin 靠什么让第一批人看见并相信它，市场后来给了它什么定价？

## Protagonist and actors
- Devin: product with visible autonomous actions (task planning, browser, code editor, terminal).
- Cognition founders Scott Wu, Steven Hao, Walden Yan: three IOI gold medalists; Wu explains why that background matters.
- Customers/proof actors: Mercedes-Benz pilot; Cognition's own engineers.
No invented protagonist with unobserved motives.

## Product actions (max three)
1. Task handoff: a user/session enters a task; Devin creates a plan.
2. Autonomous execution: Devin opens web pages, edits files, runs terminal commands; every step remains visible.
3. Parallel Devins: multiple Devin sessions run at once, each with its own cloud IDE.

## Visual language
- Use official videos as evidence, not decoration: Introducing Devin (2024-03-12), Devin 2.0/parallel sessions (2025-04-24), Mercedes official video (2026-04-27), official tutorial/overview as needed.
- For 9:16: place UI replay in a center-safe 4:5 crop; source labels in lower safe zone; numbers as burn-on text when first spoken.
- Strong facts get simultaneous on-screen evidence: product action at first mention, Mercedes number with its source, valuation with TechCrunch/official label.

## Source posture
'First AI software engineer', pricing, Mercedes result, 89% self-use and financing are company-reported or media-reported, not independent verification. The demo is official; play it as an official demonstration, not a benchmark.

## Fact boundaries
- Use only claims with allowedInNarration=true.
- Keep out: SWE-bench, PR merge rate, speed multiplier, independent test disputes, unclaimed 2025 financing, Google/GS narrative unless backed by a Claim in facts.
- Do not say 'same plan dropped from $500 to $20': those are different plans from different releases.
- Do not convert views to users or adoption.

## Why Now
No measured market-timing claim is available. The as-of durability test (official tutorials/overview still show autonomous execution in 2026) is not a growth claim; use it only as 'this remains the official product state', or omit it.

## Ending
Stop on the 2026-05-27 capital-market evidence: more than $1B raised at $2.5B pre-money / $2.6B post-money. No 'you can do it too' close.
