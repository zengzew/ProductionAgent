# Story Angle — episode-004: Devin / Cognition

## Candidate angles compared
1. Gold-medal founder origin
   - Pros: strong, sourced human background; sets up why the team is unusual.
   - Cons: no measured user pain or motivation quote; if used alone it becomes a biography and strips the product from the screen.
2. Benchmark/technical leader angle
   - Pros: SWE-bench number exists in research.
   - Cons: claim-devin-010 is not allowed in narration; a 13.86% benchmark is abstract and less believable on screen without the product working.
3. Watch the AI engineer work (chosen)
   - Pros: product promise and evidence are the same thing: a visible workspace where Devin plans, browses, edits, and runs commands. It connects directly to launch video, pricing, parallel Devins, Mercedes case, self-use and valuation.
   - Cons: requires careful use of official footage; if footage is missing, must stop and request asset research instead of substituting a landing page.

## Selected angle
Default structure: visible product result -> founder need/idea -> cold-start/attention -> market pricing.

## Story thesis
This product promises an AI teammate that completes software-engineering tasks for human review, with every step visible.
The evidence shows that promise through official demo footage, 1.22M+ views, founder statements, plan/pricing changes, a Mercedes COBOL case, 89% self-use, and a $2.5B pre-money raise.

## Why this angle wins
- The first 20 seconds can show the product doing work, which answers 'what is it' without relying on category hype.
- The chosen turning interval (Devin 2.0 pricing and parallel Devins) is product-shaped, not a version list; it changes who can access the tool.
- The ending is a sourced market price, not an open-ended question.
