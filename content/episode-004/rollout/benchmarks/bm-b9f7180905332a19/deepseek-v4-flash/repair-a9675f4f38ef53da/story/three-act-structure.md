# Three-Act Structure — episode-004: Devin / Cognition

Target: 60 seconds (40-80s allowed). Every segment adds information; no beat survives on visuals alone.

## Act I — See an AI software engineer work (0-20s)

- Time: 0-20s
- Narrative task: establish a visible product mental model before history.
- Product actions: #1 task handoff -> plan; #2 autonomous execution through browser/editor/terminal.
- Visible action:
  - 0-3s: official demo opens inside Devin's workspace; a task is already being executed; terminal output and browser panel are visible.
  - 3-10s: one small task completes on screen: Devin opens a web page, modifies a file, runs a command; labels 'browser / code editor / terminal' burn on.
  - 10-20s: launch stamp '2024-03-12' and official demo view count '1.22M+' appear with source label.
- Claim IDs: claim-devin-001, claim-devin-003, claim-devin-004, claim-devin-015.
- Source identity: Cognition official YouTube 'Introducing Devin' (2024-03-12).
- Rhythm: start mid-work (not a logo), keep cuts to actual UI actions, end on the unanswered question 'who built it and why?'
- Fact boundary: official demo, not independent evaluation; view count is attention evidence, not conversion.

Hook:
- 0-3s: 'Devin is doing a real software task, and you can watch it.'
- 3-10s: 'It has its own browser, editor and terminal; every step is visible.'
- 10-20s: 'This official demo has 1.22M+ views; who built it and what happened next?'

Information increment: result -> product shape -> attention evidence.

## Act II — Why this team, and how the product widened access (20-40s)

- Time: 20-40s
- Narrative task: answer 'why this team', then surface the turning interval.
- Product action #3: parallel Devins.
- Visible action:
  - 20-30s: three founder names/cards: Scott Wu, Steven Hao, Walden Yan; caption 'IOI gold medalists'; short Wu statement about olympiad background helps treat AI coding as complex decision-making.
  - 30-38s: official Devin 2.0 / parallel video: from one control panel, multiple Devin sessions launch; each has its own cloud IDE.
  - 38-40s: on-screen plan labels: '2024-12 GA team plan: $500/month' vs '2025-04 Devin 2.0 personal plan: from $20/month' — a source-backed correction that these are different plans.
- Claim IDs: claim-devin-012, claim-devin-013, claim-devin-005, claim-devin-006.
- Source identity: Bloomberg/Wikipedia for founders; Cognition official blog 'Devin 2.0' and official parallel video for product/pricing.
- Rhythm: slow founder beat, then faster parallel action, then price comparison on a single freeze-frame.
- Fact boundary: no adoption numbers; no 'pivot' or 'crisis'; parallel Devin is a product capability, not a success metric.
- Relationship to previous: sequence-only (founder background -> product choice; pricing/product expansion -> wider access).
- Information increment: team credibility -> product expansion -> access choice.

## Act III — Enterprise proof and market price (40-60s)

- Time: 40-60s
- Narrative task: add credibility proof, then stop on a sourced market price.
- Visible action:
  - 40-48s: Mercedes case: source label 'Cognition official blog, 2026-04-27'; on-screen numbers '200,000+ lines COBOL' and 'estimated 8 months -> 8 days'; use official Mercedes video or blog artwork with number burn.
  - 48-55s: Cognition self-use: '89% of code committed by company engineers is committed by Devin'; label 'Cognition official blog, 2026-05-27'. This is a company-reported operational proof, not a product success rate.
  - 55-60s: final capital-market beat: '> $1B raised, $2.5B pre-money / $2.6B post-money'; label 'TechCrunch / Cognition, 2026-05-27'. Hold on valuation number.
- Claim IDs: claim-devin-007, claim-devin-008, claim-devin-016.
- Source identity: Cognition official blog and YouTube for Mercedes; Cognition official blog / TechCrunch for 89% and financing.
- Rhythm: case proof -> internal proof -> one final valuation hold; no callback unless it adds new meaning, and by default it is not used.
- Fact boundary: Mercedes 8 months is an estimate, not measured before/after; 89% and financing are company/media-reported; no claim of profit, success rate, or causal link from proof to valuation.
- Relationship to previous: Mercedes time reduction is `causal` only as the Claim's stated outcome; self-use -> internal trust is sequence-only; proof -> valuation is sequence-only.
- Information increment: external customer proof -> internal self-use -> market price.
