# Story Bible — episode-004: Devin

## Canon
- Devin is Cognition's AI product positioned by the company as `the first AI software engineer` (claim-devin-002, src-devin-001/002).
- Product form: Devin receives a task, then uses a sandboxed shell, code editor, and browser; every step is visible in the official demo (claim-devin-001/003/004).
- Official workflow: engineers hand tasks to Devin and review the completed result (claim-devin-009, src-devin-001/003/015).
- Founders: Scott Wu, Steven Hao, Walden Yan; all IOI gold medalists (claim-devin-012, src-devin-016/017). Scott Wu says the algorithmic background helped teach AI complex decisions (claim-devin-013).
- Seed: Founders Fund $21M at $350M valuation around launch (claim-devin-014, src-devin-016).
- Cold start: official launch demo on YouTube had 1.22M+ views by 2026-08-16 (claim-devin-015, src-devin-002).
- GA pricing: Dec 2024 team plan $500/month, unlimited seats (claim-devin-005, src-devin-009).
- Devin 2.0: Apr 2025 individual plan from $20/month; multiple parallel Devins each with cloud IDE (claim-devin-005/006, src-devin-004/014).
- Mercedes-Benz: Apr 2026 official case: Devin analyzed 200k+ lines of COBOL; estimated modernization 8 months to 8 days in four-week pilot; then full suite deployment (claim-devin-007, src-devin-006/011).
- Self-use: Cognition reports 89% of code committed by its engineers is committed by Devin (claim-devin-008, src-devin-007/008).
- Capital: May 2026 raised $1B+ at $2.5B pre-money / $2.6B post-money (claim-devin-016, src-devin-007).

## Source identity rules
- Official blogs and YouTube: product capability, company-reported metrics, pricing, customer case.
- Bloomberg / Wikipedia: founder identities and seed financing.
- TechCrunch / VentureBeat: funding and pricing reporting; cross-check with official blogs.

## Boundaries
- Do not put SWE-bench 13.86% or 2025 PR merge-rate (34→67) in narration.
- Do not say $500 became $20. $500 is team plan at GA; $20/month is Devin 2.0 individual plan.
- `First AI software engineer` is official self-description, not third-party certification.
- Company-reported numbers must be labeled as company-reported.
- No invented user stories, no crisis, no single-point explosion, no industry-trend filler for Why Now.
- Product autonomy is a form factor, not a success-rate claim.

## Recurring evidence
- Official demo video (src-devin-002) for Devin's visible tool use.
- Official tutorial / Devin 2.0 materials (src-devin-003/004/014) for parallel Devins and cloud IDE.
- Official Mercedes video (src-devin-011) for customer case.
- YouTube view count (src-devin-002) for distribution reach, not conversion.
