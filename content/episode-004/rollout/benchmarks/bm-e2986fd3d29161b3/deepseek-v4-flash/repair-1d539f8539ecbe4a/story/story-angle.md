# Story Angle — episode-004

## Candidates
### Angle A: The whole job
Open with Devin already doing work; then founders, demo/seed, pricing turn, Mercedes/raise.
Strength: product value visible first; fits result-first structure.
Risk: need discipline not to turn into feature list.

### Angle B: The $500-to-$20 price collapse
Hook on price drop.
Strength: clear market shift.
Risk: $500 was team GA and $20 is personal 2.0; not the same plan; easy to misstate; also leads with pricing before product mental model.

### Angle C: Three gold medalists build their replacement
Founder-human-interest.
Strength: memorable maker story.
Risk: sources give only one founder statement; no psychological arc; would need inference and would delay product proof.

## Selected
Angle A: The whole job.

## Why
It puts the sourced product action on screen first, lets the Mercedes result and $25B raise answer 'what did the market price that at,' and avoids both pricing misstatement and invented founder drama.

## Story thesis
This product promises a coding teammate that completes tasks for review.
The evidence shows that promise through visible browser/editor/terminal actions, a widely viewed demo, team-to-individual pricing, and a $25B pre-money raise.

## Chain
- Need/idea: founders' stated edge (IOI/competitive programming) + official product positioning as independent task completion.
- Product action: official demo/tutorial footage of task -> browser -> editor -> terminal -> reviewable result.
- Cold start / distribution: public demo at launch (1.22M+ views) + seed financing in same window; marked sequence-only.
- Capital evidence: GA $500/mo team -> Devin 2.0 $20/mo personal -> Mercedes pilot -> $25B pre-money raise.

## Boundaries
- No user adoption or revenue claims.
- No causal link between demo views and financing.
- Do not present $500 and $20 as the same plan.
- Do not claim founder motivations beyond the sourced statement.
