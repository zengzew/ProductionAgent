# Story Bible — episode-004

## Binding
As of 2026-08-16. Bound to facts.json, sources.json, timeline.json hashes in director-brief.

## Protagonists
- Devin: the product whose visible actions carry the story.
- Cognition: developer of Devin, founded 2023.
- Scott Wu, Steven Hao, Walden Yan: founders; Scott Wu is the only founder voice used, and only for the sourced competitive-programming edge.
- Mercedes-Benz: named enterprise customer in an official case study.

## Dramatic question
Can an AI software engineer take a task and do the job, and what did the market pay for that promise?

## Promise / proof
- Promise: an AI teammate that completes tasks for a human to review.
- Proof in footage: Devin opens its own browser, edits code, runs commands; later, multiple parallel Devins run in separate cloud IDEs.
- Proof in market: $21M seed at $350M, $500/mo GA, $20/mo personal start, Mercedes 8 months to 8 days, $25B pre-money raise.

## Tension
Old behavior: coding copilots suggest code; a human still does the job.
New behavior: Devin executes the job in a sandbox and leaves a reviewable result.
Use this to explain value, not to attack competitors.

## Story spine
1. See the agent working (0-10s).
2. Name it and its makers (10-20s).
3. Show how it was seen and funded (20-30s).
4. Show the price/access turn (30-45s).
5. End on enterprise and capital proof (45-60s).

## Visual grammar
- Official demo video is the primary evidence; crop/zoom for 9:16 readability.
- Source labels on every official clip: Cognition official demo, Mar 12 2024; Cognition fan-out video, Apr 2025; Cognition-Benz video, Apr 2026.
- If a frame is unreadable at 9:16, build a labelled feature-demo graphic from the same footage; never replace with a landing page.
- Numbers must appear as text cards with source labels, not as voice-only.

## Hard boundaries
- No user counts or revenue.
- No causality from demo views to adoption/funding.
- No 'first AI software engineer' as third-party certification; attribute to Cognition.
- Mercedes 8-month baseline was an estimate; no independent audit.
- SWE-bench and PR-merge metrics stay out of narration.
- No invented founder psychology, crisis, or competitor attack.
