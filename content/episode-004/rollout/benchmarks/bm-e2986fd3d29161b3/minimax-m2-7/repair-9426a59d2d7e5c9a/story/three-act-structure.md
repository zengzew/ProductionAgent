# Three-Act Structure — Episode 004: Devin by Cognition

**Runtime target:** 60 seconds (±20s). Structure designed for TTS pacing and 9:16 format.

---

## Act 1: The Autonomous Agent (0–22 seconds)

### Narrative Task
Establish Devin as a product that executes tasks autonomously — not suggests code — using a visible demo as the hook.

### Visible Action
- **0–12s:** Devin interface from official demo or tutorial. Task is entered. Devin opens a browser, writes code, runs a terminal command. Test result appears. All steps visible in Devin's own UI.
- **12–22s:** Product name and model. "Devin by Cognition — three IOI gold medalists built it as an autonomous AI software engineer. Give it a task, it delivers a tested result for review."

### Claim IDs
- claim-devin-001, 003, 004, 009 (autonomous execution, visible steps)
- claim-devin-002 (first AI software engineer)
- claim-devin-012, 013 (founder credibility)

### New Information vs. Prior Beat
- Beat 1: No prior context — this is the hook. New: autonomous execution visible on screen.
- Beat 2: Adds product identity and founder background. New: who built it and why they were credible.

### Rhythm
- Beat 1 is all visual — no narration until the viewer has seen what Devin does.
- Beat 2 shifts to informational: name, model, credibility.
- No version numbers or release dates in this act.

---

## Act 2: From Demo to Market (22–54 seconds)

### Narrative Task
Show the product's path from viral demo to enterprise customer — the cold start signal, the pricing evolution, and the first major named deployment result.

### Visible Action
- **22–32s:** Demo views and seed data. "The official demo reached 1.22 million views. On the day of release, Founders Fund invested $21 million at a $350 million valuation." (Sourced graphic or CredibilityTag overlay)
- **32–42s:** Devin 2.0 pricing and parallel execution. "Devin 2.0 added parallel execution and a $20/month personal plan. The earlier team tier was $500/month." (Devin 2.0 feature video or parallel Devins demo)
- **42–54s:** Mercedes-Benz result. "Mercedes-Benz used Devin to analyze over 200,000 lines of COBOL code — cutting the modernization from an estimated 8 months to 8 days. They then deployed the full product suite." (Sourced graphic: 200K lines / 8 days + CredibilityTag)

### Claim IDs
- claim-devin-014, 015 (demo views, seed valuation)
- claim-devin-005, 006 (Devin 2.0 pricing, parallel execution)
- claim-devin-007 (Mercedes-Benz result)

### Relationship Markers
| Transition | Type | Evidence |
|------------|------|----------|
| Demo views → pricing evolution | sequence-only | Both sourced from public events, no direct causal claim in sources |
| Pricing → Mercedes result | causal | Official blog explicitly states Mercedes expanded from pilot to full deployment after seeing the COBOL result |

### New Information vs. Prior Beat
- Beat 3: Adds market attention signal (views, seed). New: how the world found out.
- Beat 4: Adds product evolution and pricing choice. New: deliberate access strategy.
- Beat 5: Adds enterprise validation with a named client and specific measurable outcome. New: a Fortune 500 company published a result.

### Rhythm
- Three distinct beats with clear time/evidence shifts.
- Each beat answers a different implied question (how did it spread → why did it evolve → who actually used it).
- Mercedes beat is the longest: it carries the credibility weight of the entire middle act.

---

## Act 3: Market Verdict (54–68 seconds)

### Narrative Task
Close on a capital markets result — the market's answer to what an autonomous AI software engineer is worth.

### Visible Action
- **54–68s:** Series D result. "In May 2026, Cognition raised over $1 billion at a $25 billion pre-money valuation." (Sourced graphic: $25B / $1B+ with CredibilityTag)

### Claim IDs
- claim-devin-016 (Series D valuation)

### Relationship to Prior Beat
| From | To | Type | Evidence |
|------|-----|------|----------|
| Mercedes deployment (Beat 5) | Series D valuation (Beat 6) | causal | The TechCrunch report and company blog explicitly connect the May 2026 raise to company growth and market position following enterprise adoption signals |

### New Information vs. Prior Beat
- Beat 6: Adds the capital markets conclusion — a specific dollar value the market placed on the company. New: quantifiable evidence that the product reached commercial scale.

### Rhythm
- Single beat, clean resolution.
- No question at the end. No "what's next." The $25B valuation is the answer.
- Target runtime ends at or before 68 seconds.

## Execution Rules for This Episode

1. **Open with Devin doing something** — not a logo, not a founder, not a text card. The first frame must show autonomous execution.
2. **Maximum 3 product actions across the full runtime:** (a) Devin executing a task, (b) parallel Devins running, (c) none — the Mercedes result is a company claim, not a product demo.
3. **Do not show a copilot interface** — it must be Devin's own sandboxed environment.
4. **$500/month and $20/month must not be described as a price drop** — they are different tiers for different buyers.
5. **Mercedes "8 months" must include the estimate qualifier** — it is a project estimate, not a measured before/after.
6. **Closing frame: $25B valuation sourced graphic.** No logo, no fade to black text.
