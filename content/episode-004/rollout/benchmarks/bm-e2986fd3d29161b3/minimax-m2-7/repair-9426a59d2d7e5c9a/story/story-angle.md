# Story Angle — Episode 004: Devin by Cognition

## Selected Angle: Autonomous Execution

**Devin promises to complete software engineering tasks autonomously — not suggest code, but execute, test, and deliver.**

The evidence shows that promise through an official demo that reached 1.22M views without paid promotion, a deliberate pricing expansion from enterprise teams to individual developers, a named Fortune 500 customer with a specific measured result, and a capital markets valuation of $25 billion.

## Why This Angle

1. **It is demonstrable on screen.** The official demo and tutorial videos show Devin accepting a task, opening a browser, writing code, running commands, and producing a result — all visible. No other angle offers a verifiable visual hook this strong.

2. **It matches the product's self-positioning.** From the March 2024 launch, Cognition's own language was "independently complete tasks for you to review" — not "AI-assisted coding." The autonomous execution model is the founding premise, not a later feature.

3. **It creates natural narrative tension for a zero-background viewer.** The viewer has seen autocomplete tools. The moment Devin opens its own browser and executes without being prompted at each step is the perceptional shift the story needs.

4. **It chains cleanly to all evidence types:**
   - Product behavior → visible demo (1.22M views)
   - Cold start → no paid ads; virality from demonstration
   - Enterprise proof → Mercedes (200K lines, specific time result)
   - Market pricing → $25B valuation, $1B+ raise

## Alternatives Considered and Rejected

### Angle A: Founder Credibility — "Elite Engineers Built What They Wished Existed"
- **Evidence:** IOI gold medals, Founders Fund seed on day of release
- **Rejection reason:** Credibility explains *who*, not *what*. It does not give a zero-background viewer a reason to care about the product itself. It risks becoming a founder profile rather than a product story.

### Angle B: Pricing Evolution — "From $500 to $20"
- **Evidence:** Team tier $500/month (GA Dec 2024), personal tier $20/month (Devin 2.0 Apr 2025)
- **Rejection reason:** Pricing alone is a business story, not a product story. A viewer who sees only the price change learns *when* the market expanded, not *why* the product mattered. Also risks misrepresenting the two prices as the same tier reduced in price.

## Misconception to Correct

**Potential viewer misconception:** Viewers may assume "AI software engineer" means Devin writes code that engineers then use directly — a copilot model. The 89% internal code commit figure, if mentioned in press, reinforces this.

**Correction approach:** Show the autonomous execution model from the first frame. The interface — Devin's own browser and terminal, not the engineer's IDE — is the correction. Do not lecture; demonstrate.

## Story Structure in One Sentence

Three IOI gold medalists built an AI that autonomously executes software engineering tasks; an official demo reached 1.22M views without paid ads; after expanding from a $500/month team tier to a $20/month personal plan, a Mercedes-Benz engagement produced a verifiable enterprise result — and the market priced the product at $25 billion.
