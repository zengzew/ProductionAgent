## Rhythm Notes

- Act 1 delivers product definition + cold-start proof in 20 seconds
- Act 2 delivers pricing strategy + enterprise validation in 35 seconds
- Act 3 delivers market pricing in 15 seconds
- Every 20–40 seconds has a new action, new evidence, or new question
- Maximum 3 distinct product actions: (1) demo execution, (2) Devin 2.0 UI/parallel, (3) Mercedes context
- No act contains only scale numbers without a new product action or proof
- Strong facts first appear with同期 evidence (demo video, Mercedes blog, valuation graphic)
