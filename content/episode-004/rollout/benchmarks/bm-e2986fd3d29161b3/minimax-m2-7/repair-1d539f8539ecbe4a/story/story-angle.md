# Story Angle — Devin / Cognition (episode-004)

## Chosen Angle

**Product-Capability-First: The AI that actually does the job**

Devin differentiates from code-completion and chat-based tools by autonomously executing complete coding tasks in a visible sandbox environment. The story opens with that visible execution, proves the market accepted it via a viral launch demo and $350M seed valuation, then traces distribution expansion through pricing strategy ($500/month enterprise → $20/month personal), validates the capability at enterprise scale (Mercedes COBOL), and closes with market pricing ($25B pre-money valuation).

## Why This Angle

| Candidate Angle | Strength | Weakness | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: IOI founders replace themselves | Founder story is documented | Origin anecdote gap; too meta | Rejected |
| **B: AI that actually does the job vs. AI that suggests** | **Visible execution differentiates from all copilot/chat tools; directly answered by demo (1.22M+ views)** | **Requires on-screen evidence to land** | **Selected** |
| C: Pricing strategy as distribution | Clear evolution documented | Pricing alone doesn't prove product value | Secondary |

Angle B was selected because:
1. **Visible differentiation**: Devin's sandboxed execution (browser + editor + terminal visible) answers the viewer's implicit question "why not just use GitHub Copilot?" without inventing a competitor comparison
2. **Cold-start proof exists**: 1.22M+ views on the official demo proves the product was seen and accepted at launch
3. **Chain is traceable**: demand (engineers assign/review) → visibility (demo views) → pricing expansion (Devin 2.0) → enterprise validation (Mercedes) → market pricing ($25B)
4. **No invented pivot**: story moves through documented events without needing a founder crisis or direction change

## What This Angle Demands

- Official demo video must enter before 10 seconds to establish autonomous execution visually
- Mercedes official video or blog must enter with concrete numbers (200K lines, 8 days vs. estimated months)
- Valuation figure must enter as the final evidence, not a throwaway stat

## What This Angle Forbids

- No origin anecdote ("one day Scott Wu thought...") — the IOI background is stated as a fact, not dramatized
- No manufactured failure or crisis — story proceeds through documented strategic choices
- No "回看开场" ending — story stops at market pricing
- No SWE-bench or internal metrics narrated

## Story Thesis

> Devin promises to hand off repetitive coding tasks to an autonomous AI teammate so engineers focus on review and decisions.
>
> The evidence shows that promise through:
> 1. an official demo proving autonomous execution capability on launch day (1.22M+ views)
> 2. pricing evolution from enterprise-only ($500/month) to personal ($20/month) expanding the user base
> 3. Mercedes-Benz validating enterprise-scale deployment with a concrete COBOL modernization result
> 4. a $25B pre-money valuation confirming market pricing

## Tension Source

The story's natural tension comes from the gap between "AI executes autonomously" and "AI is trustworthy enough for real work." Each beat answers this:

| Beat | Tension Resolution |
|---|---|
| Hook | Show it: autonomous action on screen |
| Demand | Frame it: engineer assigns, reviews result |
| Cold Start | Prove others saw it: 1.22M+ views, $350M valuation |
| Pricing Pivot | Expand access: $20/month personal |
| Enterprise Validation | Scale it: Mercedes case |
| Capital Validation | Price it: $25B valuation |

## Misconception to Correct

**Natural audience misconception**: "AI coding tools all do the same thing—Copilot has been doing this for years."

**Correction (sourced)**: Devin's autonomous execution differs from suggestion-based tools. Copilot suggests next lines; Devin plans a task, uses a browser and terminal, and delivers a completed pull request for review. The Mercedes case proves this works at enterprise scale, not just in a demo.

This correction is scheduled for the enterprise validation beat, after the viewer has already seen the product definition and understands the distinction.

## Causal vs. Sequence-Only Links

| From | To | Type | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demo visibility | Seed valuation | sequence-only | Both are documented at launch; no causal claim made |
| Pricing drop to $20/month | Expanded access | causal | Price is documented distribution mechanism |
| Mercedes deployment | Enterprise credibility | causal | Documented reference customer |
| Enterprise credibility | $25B valuation | sequence-only | Valuation is market pricing; internal reasoning not documented |
