Frequently asked questions

What is AI product photography?

AI product photography uses artificial intelligence to edit, enhance, and generate professional product photos automatically. With Photoroom, you can remove backgrounds, adjust lighting, and create realistic settings: no studio or retouching experience required.

Can AI product photography replace traditional studio shoots?

Yes, in many cases AI product photography can replace traditional shoots. Photoroom recreates studio-quality lighting, shadows, and realistic environments digitally, helping businesses cut production costs and launch visuals faster.

Do I need professional equipment to use Photoroom for AI product photography?

No, you don’t need a studio or expensive camera. Photoroom works with any photo, even those taken on a smartphone, and uses AI to deliver polished, professional results in seconds.

How can AI product photography help e‑commerce brands scale content production?

AI product photography automates repetitive editing and staging tasks. With Photoroom’s Batch Mode and APIs, brands can process hundreds of images at once, ensuring every visual stays consistent and ready for marketplace or campaign use.

Is AI product photography suitable for all industries?

Yes, AI product photography works across all categories, from fashion and beauty to furniture, food, and retails. Photoroom adapts to each product type, creating visuals that fit your brand style and platform requirements.

Can I automate AI product photography with the Photoroom API?

Yes. The Photoroom API gives you programmatic access to background removal, retouching, scene generation and batch exports, so you can plug it into your PIM, DAM or e‑commerce platform and process new SKUs as they're added rather than in manual batches. Enterprise plans include a single usage wallet across the app, web and API, with dedicated integration support.
